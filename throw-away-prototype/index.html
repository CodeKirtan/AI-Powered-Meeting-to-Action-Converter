📋 Requirements Alignment & Stakeholder Guide (Click to expand) ▲

🎯 Purpose of this Throw-Away Prototype

Throw-away prototyping is intended to clarify user requirements and validate workflows with stakeholders before committing to architecture, production DBs, or complex LLM fine-tuning. It will be discarded once requirements are confirmed.

👥 Team Responsibilities:

  • Neel: Overall flow, stepper architecture, requirements alignment (FR-01 to FR-24).
  • Kirtan: Meeting creation (FR-02) and transcript input screens (FR-01, FR-24).
  • Smit: AI extraction review interface, uncertainty/confidence handling (FR-03 to FR-15).
  • Arnav: Task board status management (FR-16 to FR-19), approval flow, notifications (FR-20 to FR-22).

💡 Stakeholder Validation Questions to Ask:

  1. Do users prefer reviewing all tasks one-by-one or using "Approve All High Confidence"?
  2. Is the low-confidence warning clear enough when an owner or deadline is missing?
  3. Should WhatsApp alerts be sent immediately upon approval, or batched as a daily digest?
  4. Are key decisions and unresolved questions useful alongside action items?
